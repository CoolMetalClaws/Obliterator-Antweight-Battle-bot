# Obliterator - Antweight battle bot

Obliterator is a 150g Antweight cobat robot with a flat body, an aggressive design, and a large horizontal weapon spinning at speeds of 25,000 rpm.  Haven’t heard of combat robotics before? It’s basically custom RC cars with weapons trying to destroy each other! This bot is my second antweight bot, with more to come!

## Why i built this

This project is my second combat robot, built for an upcoming competition using all the knowledge i gained from my previous model, Cataclysm. I was unhappy with how Cataclysm performed at the last event, so i have figured out everything that went wrong, and improved it when implementing it into Obliterator. I thoroughly enjoy the hobby of combat robotics, so i will be working on more bots in the future as well!

## Parts 

### - Chassis

The main chassis of the bot with screw on N20 motor mounts, a mount for the Llama Forge control board, the ESC, the battery, and wiring. Designed with a flat design, low set wheels, and thicker walls with angular facets.

### - Replaceable Wheel Guards

Quick swap wheel guards that are easy and fast to replace, designed to protect the wheel from hard hits and prevent it from slipping off, especially when it takes hard hits. Their shape is currently mostly aesthetic, and will be adapted after testing.

### - Weapon Blade 

A 75mm diameter, single toothed weapon blade spinning at 25,000 RPM mounted between 2 plates, 10mm thick. Designed to fling and destroy the opponents with ease. 

### - Top Plate 

A sturdy top plate that provides a cover for the main chassis and a firm support for the weapon.

### - Wheels

Small N20 compatible wheels utilising O rings for extra grip.
