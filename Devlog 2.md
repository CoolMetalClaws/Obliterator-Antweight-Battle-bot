# Bristless!!

I added some very spiky and long bristles to the back of my bot, to add a thick line of defense behind the bot, to protect it from hard hits while remaining extremely light, unlike a solid extention.

## Screw Switch 

A slot for the screw switch was also added, so i can turn the bot on and off from the outside, and ensuring that the switch cant accidentally be switched off in a battle.
